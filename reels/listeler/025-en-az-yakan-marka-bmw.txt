2. BMW 1 Serisi F40 (116d 116 HP Manuel): 4,0 L/100 km
3. BMW 2 Serisi F22 (218d 150 HP Steptronic): 4,0 L/100 km
4. BMW X1 F48 (sDrive16d 116 HP Manuel): 4,0 L/100 km
5. BMW 5 Serisi G30 (520d Hafif Hibrit 190 HP Steptronic): 4,2 L/100 km
