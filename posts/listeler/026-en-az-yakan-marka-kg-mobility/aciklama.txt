1. KG Mobility Tivoli (1.6 VGT 115 HP Manuel): 4,3 L
2. KG Mobility Korando (2.2 e-XDi 178 HP Manuel): 5,3 L
3. KG Mobility Rexton (RX 220 e-XDi 178 HP Manuel): 6,8 L
4. KG Mobility Musso (2.2 e-XDi 181 HP Manuel): 7,6 L
5. KG Mobility Torres (1.5 T-GDi 163 HP Manuel): 7,9 L
